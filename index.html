Fatigue Journal Support

Fatigue Journal helps you log fatigue during the day and review patterns over time. You can also track notes, sleep, exercise, and custom metrics.

Where is my data stored?

Your journal is stored locally in the app on your device. You can export a CSV or PDF copy from the app.

How do I remove my data?

Open Settings in Fatigue Journal and choose Clear All Data.

How do I turn reminders off?

Open Settings in the app and turn off reminders, or change notification permission in iOS Settings.
