Fatigue Journal Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Fatigue Journal is a personal fatigue-tracking app. It does not require an account.

Information you enter

Fatigue ratings, notes, dates, and metrics you add (such as sleep or exercise) are stored in the app's local database on your device. App preferences, including reminder and chart settings, are stored on the device as well. Fatigue Journal does not send this journal information to a server.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, the app schedules local notifications on your device. You can turn reminders off in Settings or in iOS notification settings.

Import and export

You can choose a CSV file to import and export your records as CSV or PDF. These actions happen when you choose them; Fatigue Journal does not upload the files to a server.

Optional tips

If you choose to leave an optional tip, Apple processes the in-app purchase through the App Store. Fatigue Journal does not receive your payment card details. Apple's handling of purchase information is described in Apple's privacy policy.

Analytics and advertising

Fatigue Journal does not include third-party analytics or advertising services.

Your choices

You can delete the app's saved journal records from Settings using Clear All Data. You can also manage notification permission in iOS Settings. Files you export are managed by you outside the app.

Contact

For privacy questions, email kinateder@gmail.com.

Changes

This policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change. The latest version will be available at the privacy policy URL listed on the App Store.
